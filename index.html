About us

A family gathered by grace.

Truthway Fellowship is a loving, Bible-believing church family.

We are a Christ-centered family gathered by grace — worshipping in spirit and truth, praying together, and serving our community with the love of Jesus.

Our vision

Lives transformed

To see lives transformed by the gospel and rooted deeply in the Word of God.

Our mission

Truth, faith, unity

To walk in truth, grow in faith, and unite people in Christ.
